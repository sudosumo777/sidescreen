Privacy policy

SideScreen · last updated 14 October 2026

SideScreen is a dashboard that runs entirely on your own Windows PC. It has no servers, no accounts of its own, no analytics and no advertising. This page explains what it does with the data of a Google account you connect to it.

What it accesses

Only what you allow on Google's own consent page, and only for the account you choose there:

  • YouTube (manage your YouTube account): your playlists, liked videos, subscriptions and the new videos of the channels you follow; it likes a video, saves it to a playlist or subscribes to a channel only when you tap the matching button.
  • Google Calendar (read only): your upcoming events.
  • Gmail (metadata only): the sender, subject, date and labels of your messages. SideScreen cannot read the content of a message or its attachments.
  • Google Tasks: your task lists; it ticks a task off, puts it back or adds one only when you ask it to.

How it uses it

Solely to show this information to you on your own screen and to carry out the actions you request. SideScreen's voice assistant runs on your PC too: it can read this information to answer you, and it can learn from your own conversations with it, on your PC only, to understand you better. Google user data is never used for advertising, never sold, and never used to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine-learning models.

Where it is stored

Only on your PC. The keys Google gives SideScreen are encrypted with Windows' own data protection, tied to your Windows account; the information it shows is kept in SideScreen's folder on your PC so the screen can show it again quickly. Nothing is uploaded anywhere: SideScreen talks only to Google's own services, directly from your PC.

Who it is shared with

No one. SideScreen does not transfer Google user data to any third party, and no person other than you sees it.

How to remove it

Disconnect the account in SideScreen's settings, which deletes its keys from your PC, or remove SideScreen's access at any time from your Google Account. Deleting SideScreen's folders from your PC removes everything it kept.

Google API Services User Data Policy

SideScreen's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
