SideScreen

A personal touch dashboard for a second screen beside a Windows PC.

SideScreen runs on your own computer and fills a small touch screen with what you want at a glance: media, your calendar, your mail and tasks, markets, the PC's health, a timer, and an on-device voice assistant. It has no servers and no accounts of its own: everything it shows is fetched and kept on your PC.

What it does with your Google account

When you choose to connect a Google account, SideScreen uses it only to show and act on your own data, on your own screen:

  • YouTube: your playlists, liked videos and the new videos of the channels you follow; Like, Save to a playlist and Subscribe when you tap them.
  • Google Calendar: your upcoming events (read only).
  • Gmail: who wrote to you and the subject line of new mail (never the content of a message).
  • Google Tasks: your task lists; ticking a task off, or adding one, when you ask it to.

You can disconnect at any time in SideScreen's settings, or remove its access from your Google Account.

Privacy

Read the privacy policy: in short, your data never leaves your PC.
